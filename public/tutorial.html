Two minutes, using made-up cards

The cards in this tour are made up and are not saved to your collection. At the end you can name your first binder — skip it anytime.

First: how are you planning to use Grimoire?

You can change this anytime in Settings → Preferences.

Add a card

Search a real card — this looks up the actual card database.

Magic cards get reprinted many times over the years, sometimes with different art or as a shiny "foil" version — each specific version is called a printing. Pick one from the list that appears after you search. On the real Receive page later you'll do the same: choose the printing that matches the physical card in your hand, so you never add the wrong (and sometimes far pricier) one.

That card box shows its name, which printing was picked, its rarity, and its current market price — the same information shown everywhere else in Grimoire.

Selling a card

"Listed" just means offered for sale — like a price tag, not a shipment. The card stays right where it is until someone actually buys it. Mark a card "listed for sale" — press Mark listed on the row below.

Once you connect a marketplace (Settings → Integrations), Grimoire keeps this in sync automatically instead of you tracking it by hand.

Selling cards is part of Grimoire's business setup, so you won't see a "Mark listed" button while you're in Simple mode. That keeps things uncluttered if you only collect or sell the odd card.

If you do want to track what's for sale, go to Settings → Preferences and untick Simple mode — hide advanced/business features. The listing buttons and the marketplace connections (Settings → Integrations) appear right away.
