Putaway rules

Pick ALL (AND) or ANY (OR) of the conditions below, with an optional NOT to invert the whole box — Color identity is exactly R + Rarity is any of common, uncommon under ALL reads exactly the way it sounds. Click + Add group to nest a second box with its own ALL/ANY/NOT, e.g. (Set is neo OR Set is mh3) AND Rarity is common. The list always shows "Evaluates as: …" above the conditions so you can check the expression it actually produced. Switch a condition off with its checkbox to park it without deleting it. A location with putaway on and no conditions matches everything — that is your overflow box, and it loses to any location with a real rule. When two locations match equally well, the higher priority wins, then the one with more free space.

The two ceilings above are what make overflow work, and they are checked separately: card ceiling caps the whole place, and max copies of the same card caps one card within it — counted across every printing and language, so a foil and a Japanese copy count against the same limit. Set it to 1 on a one-of-each binder and the second copy onward falls through to whatever ranks next, which is how a sell overflow bin gets fed: give that bin putaway with no rules and a low priority, and it quietly catches everything the nice binders will not take.
