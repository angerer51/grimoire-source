Schedule

Runs on the server, whether or not the app is open. The rule you pick must be switched on in the table above. More in Help

Auto-push to Manapool

With this on, a scheduled run updates the price of your real, already-listed Manapool listings with nobody reviewing it first. Never list below my minimum profit is on, so it also takes down listings whose new price is under your minimum profit, up to 50 a run. Any others wait, unchanged, for the next run. Those taken down stay down until you list them again from Listings. Off, a run sends nothing and only reports how many have a new price.

Danger zone

Delist everything from Manapool. Reads your live Manapool listings first, then sets every one of them to 0 on your real Manapool account: cards and sealed, including listings made on Manapool directly or that Grimoire doesn't know about, regardless of what's ticked in Evaluate. This is account-wide and immediate — there is no undo for the live listings themselves (Grimoire's own local records can still be re-pushed afterward).
